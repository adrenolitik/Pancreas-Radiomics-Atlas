Reference radiomic signatures of the normal pancreas

A pancreas-specific view of the RADAPT radiomics atlas: shape, first-order and texture features extracted with PyRadiomics from the pancreas segmentations of healthy adults. Every feature is reported with its distribution in both CT phases, so any measurement can be read against a normative range.

Cohort and provenance

Adults with no pathology reported on abdominal CT, segmented automatically with TotalSegmentator and reviewed by a senior radiology resident. Radiomic features were extracted with PyRadiomics after 4 × 4 × 4 mm voxel resampling.

Pancreas cohort

Patients522

A patient can contribute to both phases; 381 patients have a pancreas segmentation in both.

Feature composition

10 filter families: the original image, one Laplacian-of-Gaussian scale (σ = 5 mm) and eight wavelet decompositions.

Pancreatic morphometry

Shape features of the pancreas gland, as median [interquartile range]. Volumes and surfaces are measured on the resampled 4 mm mask, so they are comparable across studies rather than absolute volumetry.

Source and licence

Derived from the RADAPT radiomics atlas (Elisavet Kapetanou, University of Crete), which covers 53 abdominal and pelvic structures in 531 individuals. This repository curates the pancreas only and adds the normative tables above.
